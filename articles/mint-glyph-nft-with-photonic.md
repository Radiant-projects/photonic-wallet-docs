> **Short answer:** Prepare your file, open Mint in Photonic, fill in name and details, review the fee, and confirm.

---

## You'll see this if

- You want your first NFT
- You create collection items
- You test minting

## Why it happens

Minting writes token data in Radiant transactions, which costs network fees. More background lives in our guide on [dmint explained for radiant users](dmint-explained.md) and on the [minting guide](https://photonicwallet.org/guides/mint-glyph-tokens).

## Fix it step by step

1. Fund the wallet with some RXD.
2. Open the mint screen.
3. Add file and metadata.
4. Review the fee preview and confirm.

## If that didn't work

If minting fails, check balance and file size, then retry after sync. The [Glyph features](https://photonicwallet.org/features/glyph-tokens) covers related cases, and [Avoid losing Glyph tokens by mistake](avoid-burning-glyph-tokens.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Test with a small item before a full collection.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.
